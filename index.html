Who We Are

About Allegia Wealth

A family-first financial planning firm built on a simple idea: do what is right for the client, every time.

Home/About Us

Allegia Wealth Management was founded after Darren Colananni saw firsthand what happens when advice is driven by sales instead of what is actually right for the client.

His own family experienced it. The recommendations they received were good for the advisor, not for them. He made it his mission to make sure that never happens under his watch — and Allegia was built around that promise.

Our story

Today the focus is simple: do what is right, dig deeper, build real relationships, and deliver a plan that truly serves the client. We work with successful individuals and families who have done well financially but don't always feel that their money is working together. Investments, retirement, and everyday decisions can feel disconnected, making it hard to see the bigger picture.

We look beyond the numbers to understand what matters most to you — your life, your family, and what you are working toward. Then we build a complete Family Financial Plan that brings everything together, so you can understand how each decision supports where you are headed.
